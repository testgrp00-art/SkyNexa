Privacy policy

Effective 15 October 2026 · SkyNexa Desktop PWA 1.1

SkyNexa is an offline-capable city-building game by Nitai Studio. Support: nitai.grp00@gmail.com.

Your city stays on your device

Buildings, city name, progress and preferences are stored in your browser profile using localStorage. The app shell and installation icons are cached locally by a service worker. There is no game account or cloud-save server. Clearing browser/app data may remove your city; export a backup first.

Backups and images

Import reads the backup file you choose. Export downloads a city JSON backup or a PNG city image. The game does not send these files to Nitai Studio or a game backend.

Connections and hosting

The hosted app downloads its code and install resources and checks for app updates. A hosting provider may process standard request information such as IP address and browser metadata under that provider’s policy. This game includes no advertising, analytics or tracking SDK.

Permissions and support

SkyNexa does not request microphone, camera, location or contacts. Support opens your email app only when you choose Contact support; any message and information you send are handled by your email provider and Nitai Studio to answer that request.

Your controls

Disable sound, export a backup, replace your current city, clear the app’s site data or uninstall the PWA through your browser or Windows. Uninstall behavior depends on the platform; removing the app alone may retain browser data. Contact support for questions about this policy.
